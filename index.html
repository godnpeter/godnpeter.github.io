Dongyoon Hwang

        

Hi! I am a Ph.D. candidate at KAIST AI (DAVIAN Lab), advised by Jaegul Choo.

I am interested in building general-purpose robots that can reliably perceive, reason, and act in the unstructured physical world. My research centers on two directions: (i) grounding robot foundation models in explicit 3D geometry to connect perception with action, and (ii) developing scalable and data-efficient reinforcement learning methods that enable robots to acquire robust control policies through interaction with their environments.

Previously, I was a research intern at Krafton AI and Naver Labs Europe, and received my B.S. in Computer Science from Korea University. In December 2026, I will join Microsoft Research Asia in Tokyo as a research intern.
